# HKFRS 18 — Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements

## Source and timing

- Primary Hong Kong source: [HKICPA HKFRS 18 technical resources](https://www.hkicpa.org.hk/en/Standards-setting/Standards/New-and-major-standards/New-and-Major-Standards/HKFRS18).
- The [IFRS Foundation's IFRS 18 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/) explains the defined subtotals and disclosure changes.
- HKFRS 18 applies to financial periods beginning on or after **1 January 2027**. Earlier application is permitted. It supersedes HKAS 1 and amends other standards, including HKAS 7 and HKAS 8.
- For a question about a 2026 reporting period, do not assume early adoption. Ask whether the entity has chosen early application before presenting HKFRS 18 as its current policy.

## What changes

- HKFRS 18 adds defined categories and subtotals in the statement of profit or loss. The subtotals include **operating profit** and **profit before financing and income taxes**.
- It introduces disclosure requirements for **management-defined performance measures** and principles for aggregation, disaggregation, and where information appears in primary statements or notes.
- These are presentation and disclosure changes. Do not claim that HKFRS 18 itself changes the measurement of an account balance.

## Practical response pattern

1. State the effective date and whether early adoption has been confirmed.
2. Explain the change at the level requested, referring to the HKICPA source above.
3. For an MPL, GPL, or EIC implementation question, identify which statement layouts, mappings, comparative figures, disclosures, and review controls may need assessment. Do not assign categories to particular company accounts without the chart of accounts and accounting policy review.
4. Treat any proposed financial statement redesign as a draft for finance review. Do not post entries or approve classification from a chat answer.

For detailed transition and exceptions, consult the [HKICPA resources](https://www.hkicpa.org.hk/en/Standards-setting/Standards/New-and-major-standards/New-and-Major-Standards/HKFRS18) and the issued standard before giving a definitive conclusion.
