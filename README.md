# Hong Kong Finance Skills

A collection of AI agent skills for Hong Kong accounting, financial reporting, tax, and internal audit work. The skills are designed to help agents follow structured workflows, identify relevant requirements, and produce reviewable work products.

## Scope

The collection covers:

- Hong Kong financial reporting under HKFRS/HKAS, with relevant IFRS/IAS references
- Hong Kong profits tax and salaries tax, plus tax planning
- Internal audit and risk assessment
- Financial operations including close management, reconciliations, consolidation, budgeting, treasury, working capital, inventory, and fixed assets
- Supporting work including journal entries, financial analysis, statement preparation, and audit checklists

## Use

Use the skill relevant to the task. An AI agent should read that skill's `SKILL.md` and follow its workflow, adapting it to the facts and source documents provided. Verify accounting, tax, and regulatory conclusions against current authoritative sources before relying on them.

These skills are workflow aids and do not replace professional judgment or engagement-specific review.
